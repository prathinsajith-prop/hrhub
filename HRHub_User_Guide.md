# HRHub — User Guide

> A plain-language guide to using HRHub. Written for everyday users — employees, managers, and HR/admin staff. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## Welcome to HRHub

HRHub is your company's all-in-one HR system. Depending on your role, you can use it to:

- See your profile, payslips, leave balance, attendance, and training.
- Request leave, loans, travel, or raise a confidential complaint.
- (Managers) Approve your team's requests.
- (HR/Admin) Manage employees, payroll, visas, documents, recruitment, onboarding, and more.

HRHub works in **English and Arabic** (the layout flips for Arabic). Each company's data is completely private and separate from every other company on the platform.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Signing in
1. Open your company's HRHub link and enter your **email and password**.
2. If your company uses **two-factor authentication (2FA)**, you'll also enter a 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
3. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password** — you'll get an email with a reset link (valid for 1 hour).

> After 5 wrong password attempts, your account locks for 15 minutes for safety. Just wait and try again, or contact HR.

### Setting up 2FA (recommended)
Go to **Settings → Security**, choose **Enable 2FA**, scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.), and enter the code to confirm. **Save your backup codes** somewhere safe — they let you sign in if you lose your phone.

### Finding your way around
- **Dashboard** — your home screen with key information and shortcuts (what you see depends on your role).
- **Sidebar** — links to every area you have access to.
- **Bell icon** — your notifications (updates every minute).
- **Language switch** — toggle English/Arabic.
- **Profile menu** — edit your profile, change password, manage notifications, sign out.

### Notifications & emails
HRHub keeps you informed two ways:
- **In-app notifications** (the bell) — e.g. "Your leave was approved."
- **Emails** — e.g. payslip ready, leave decision, document verified.

You can choose what you receive in **Settings → Notifications**. (Security emails like password resets always come through.)

## 2. Who Can Do What (Roles)

HRHub has five roles. Yours decides what you can see and do.

| Role | What they typically do |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | See and manage their own information; request leave, loans, travel; raise complaints; view org chart. |
| **Department Head** | Everything an employee can, plus approve their own team's leave and see their team's data. |
| **PRO Officer** | Focus on visas, documents, and compliance. |
| **HR Manager** | Run the whole HR system — employees, payroll, recruitment, etc. |
| **Super Admin** | Everything HR can do, plus manage members, billing, and company settings. |

If you can't see a feature in this guide, your role probably doesn't include it — that's normal.

## 3. For Everyone (Self-Service)

### My Profile
**My Profile** shows your personal and job details. You can update a few fields yourself (phone, personal email, emergency contact, home address). Changes to sensitive details (like bank info) may go to HR for approval — you'll be notified when they're approved or need changes. Your work **schedule** (shift hours and weekly off) lives under **My Work → Schedule**.

### Leave
1. Go to **My Leave** and click **Request Leave**.
2. Pick the leave type, dates, and add a note.
3. Submit — your manager/HR is notified by email and in-app.
4. You'll get a notification and email when it's **approved or rejected**.

Your **leave balance** shows what you've earned, taken, and have remaining. Public holidays and weekends aren't counted as leave days.

### Payslips
**My Payslips** has two tabs: **Payslip history** lists your monthly payslips — open one to see the breakdown (basic, allowances, deductions, net pay) and download it as a PDF; **Bank details** shows the account your salary lands in (edits route through HR approval). You'll get an email when a new payslip is ready.

### Attendance
**My Attendance** shows your daily record. Depending on your company's setup you may **check in/out** from here, or attendance may come from a biometric device. The monthly calendar shows present/late/leave/holiday days.

### Training
**My Training** lists courses you've been enrolled in, with status and certificates. You'll be notified when you're assigned training, and reminded before a certificate expires.

### Loans
Request a salary loan from **My Loans**: enter the amount and monthly repayment. HR reviews it; you're notified when **approved or rejected**. Once active, repayments are automatically deducted from your payroll, and you can track the remaining balance.

### Complaints (Confidential)
Raise a workplace concern from **My Complaints**. You choose how confidential it is (named, confidential, or anonymous). Your description is stored securely. HR is alerted immediately, and you're notified when it's acknowledged and resolved. Each complaint has a target response time based on its severity.

### Announcements & Posts
Company news appears in **Announcements**, targeted to the right people. Some announcements ask you to **acknowledge** that you've read them — please do when prompted. If your company has employee posting enabled, the Home page also has a **Posts** tab where you can share an update with colleagues and read what others have posted.

### Careers & Referrals
Your company may have a public **careers page** you can share (`/careers/<company code>/jobs`) where candidates browse roles and apply with a résumé. You can also **refer** someone for an open role from the portal.

## 4. For Managers (Department Heads)

You see and act only on **your own team**.

- **Approvals** — when a team member requests leave, you're notified. Open the request and approve or reject it (you can't approve your own).
- **Team view** — see your team's profiles, attendance, and performance.
- **Performance** — create and complete reviews for your team.

## 5. For HR & Admin

### Employees
Add employees one at a time or **bulk-import** many via a spreadsheet. Maintain each employee's personal, job, salary, visa, and document details. You can record **transfers**, **salary revisions**, dependents, notes, and warnings. Archiving an employee removes them from active lists (and from their teams) while keeping history for the record.

### Recruitment
- Post jobs (draft → open → closed/on-hold) and share them on the public careers page.
- Track candidates on a **drag-and-drop pipeline** (received → screening → interview → offer → hired).
- Each candidate shows where they came from: **careers site, referral, or added by HR**.
- Schedule interviews — the candidate and interviewer get an email.
- When ready, **convert a candidate into an employee** — this automatically starts their onboarding checklist.
- Curate the **skills and qualifications** suggestion lists in **Org Settings → Recruitment** — these power the type-ahead when creating jobs, so everyone tags roles consistently. Deleting an entry only removes the suggestion; existing jobs and candidates keep their tags.

### Onboarding
New hires get a checklist (e.g. collect documents, IT setup, induction). You can email the new hire a secure **upload link** so they can submit documents before day one. When every step is complete, the employee is automatically marked **active** and welcomed.

### Payroll
Run monthly, WPS-compliant payroll:
1. Create the run for the month and review the **readiness check** (it flags missing salaries or IBANs).
2. **Process** it — payslips are generated, and employees receive their payslip by email.
3. Export the **WPS file** for the bank and mark it submitted.

Payroll automatically includes **unpaid-leave deductions** and **active loan repayments**. The tool also calculates **end-of-service gratuity** per UAE law.

### Visas
Manage the **8-step UAE visa process** per employee, record costs at each step, and export a PRO cost report. HRHub automatically reminds HR/PRO by email and in-app as visas approach expiry (90/60/30/14/7 days).

### Documents
Upload, categorize, and verify employee documents. When you **verify** a document like a passport or visa, its number and expiry date automatically update the employee's record. Everyone is reminded before documents expire.

### Compliance
A dashboard shows your standing on **WPS submissions, Emiratisation (UAE-national target), visa validity, and document completeness**, with an overall score.

### Performance
Run review cycles with ratings and feedback. Employees are notified and emailed when their review is submitted or completed.

### Assets
Track company assets (laptops, phones, etc.) and assign them to employees (who get notified). Record returns and maintenance.

### Travel
Employees submit travel requests; HR approves/rejects (everyone is notified by email and in-app). Expenses are tracked per trip and reimbursed.

### Exit & Offboarding
Start an exit, and HRHub calculates the **final settlement** (gratuity + leave encashment + unpaid salary − deductions). Track clearance tasks and an exit-interview survey. Approving the exit marks the employee as terminated.

### Organization Setup
- **Org structure** — set up Branches → Divisions → Departments, plus teams, designations, and grades.
- **Settings** — company profile, leave rules, public holidays, privacy options, and a master **notifications on/off** switch.
- **Members** — invite people to HRHub and set their roles. (Role changes and removals notify the person.)
- **Subscription** — view your plan and upgrade. If a paid plan lapses, you can keep your current staff but must renew to add more.
- **Connected Apps** — issue secure API keys (e.g. for a biometric attendance device), each limited to specific permissions.
- **Reports** — headcount, payroll, turnover, leave, and more, exportable to CSV/PDF.
- **Audit Log & Login History** — a full record of who did what and when.

## 6. What You'll Be Notified About

| When this happens… | You get… |
|---|---|
| Your leave is approved/rejected | In-app + email |
| Your payslip is ready | Email |
| You're assigned training / a cert is expiring | In-app + email |
| Your loan is approved/rejected | In-app |
| Your document is verified/rejected | In-app + email |
| Your complaint is acknowledged/resolved | In-app (+ email on resolve) |
| You're transferred | In-app + email |
| Your travel request is decided | In-app + email |
| (HR) New job application / new complaint / SLA breach | In-app + email |
| (HR/PRO) Visa, document, contract, or passport nearing expiry | In-app + email |

Your company can turn off operational notifications entirely; essential security emails still come through.

## 7. Tips & Good Habits

- **Turn on 2FA** and save your backup codes.
- **Keep your profile current** — especially contact and emergency details.
- **Check the bell** regularly for approvals and updates.
- **Acknowledge announcements** when asked.
- HR: run the **payroll readiness check** before processing, and keep employee IBANs up to date for WPS.

---

## 8. Glossary

- **2FA** — Two-factor authentication; a one-time code in addition to your password.
- **WPS** — Wages Protection System; the UAE's bank salary-transfer standard.
- **Gratuity (EOSB)** — End-of-service benefit owed when an employee leaves.
- **Pipeline** — The recruitment stages a candidate moves through.
- **Emiratisation** — UAE policy targeting a share of UAE-national employees.
- **PRO** — Public Relations Officer; handles government/visa processes.
- **Tenant** — Your company's private space in HRHub.
- **Audit log** — The record of all actions taken in the system.
